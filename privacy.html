Privacy Policy

My Own Private Radio for Mac

SupportPrivacy Policy
In short: the app collects no data. It has no server and no analytics, and nothing you do in it is sent to the developer.

What the app does with your information

The app works only on your behalf, and only with services you choose:

  • Google NotebookLM. You sign in on Google’s own page, shown inside the app. The app reads your list of notebooks and downloads your own Audio Overviews. It never sees or stores your Google password.
  • Your storage (Dropbox, or an S3-compatible service such as Backblaze B2). The app uploads your episodes and podcast feed there. Your storage keys and Dropbox connection are kept in your Mac’s Keychain.
  • Podcast services you turn on (Overcast, WebSub, Podcast Index). When your feed changes, the app tells them, which shares your feed’s address with them. If you make your feed public, Podcast Index passes its address to the public Podping network.

Your settings and saved audio stay on your Mac, in the app’s own folder.

Your feed

Unless you make it public, your feed has an unlisted address and asks podcast directories not to list it. An unlisted address is not a password: anyone who has it can listen.

Children

The app doesn’t collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/misterwindupbird/my-own-private-radio/issues.
