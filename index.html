My Own Private Radio

Your NotebookLM Audio Overviews, as a private podcast.

SupportPrivacy Policy
Need help? Open an issue at github.com/misterwindupbird/my-own-private-radio/issues. Describe what you expected and what happened. Please don’t include your feed address, keys or passwords: anyone who has a feed address can listen to it.

Getting started

  1. Sign in to NotebookLM in the app with the Google account you use for NotebookLM. If Google offers a passkey, choose “Try another way”: passkeys don’t work inside apps.
  2. Choose storage. Dropbox is quickest: click Connect Dropbox. Or use S3-compatible storage (Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3) with a bucket made for the podcast and public read access.
  3. Start publishing. The feed appears with a short welcome episode. Click Subscribe, or scan the QR code with your phone.

Common questions

A new Audio Overview hasn’t appeared yet

The app checks NotebookLM every 15 minutes while it’s running (you can change this in Settings › General), and ⌘R checks now. Only finished Audio Overviews are published. Overcast is told about new episodes at once; Apple Podcasts checks feeds on its own schedule, usually within a few hours.

The app says to sign in again

Google sometimes ends sessions. Click Sign In and sign in again; anything already saved keeps publishing meanwhile.

Can I move my podcast to other storage?

Yes. Change it in Settings › Storage and the app moves every episode, then tells podcast apps the new address so subscribers follow automatically.

Is my podcast private?

By default the feed has an unlisted address and asks directories not to list it. That isn’t a password: anyone you give the address to can listen. You can make a feed public in Settings › Sharing.

Dropbox links stopped working

Dropbox Basic allows 20 GB of downloads a day across your shared links and pauses them for a day if that’s exceeded. Paid Dropbox plans allow far more, or move the podcast to S3-compatible storage.
